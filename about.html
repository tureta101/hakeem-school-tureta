About Our School

Hakeem Nursery and Primary School Tureta, was established in 2015 to provide quality Education that has builds strong moral values, creativity, and academic excellence in young learners.

Our Vision

To nurture confident, responsible, and lifelong learners who will contribute positively for the development of our society.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, caring and stimulating environment where children receive quality education through modern teaching methods and modern instructional materials.
